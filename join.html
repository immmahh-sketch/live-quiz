Part 2: How the night works

The idea in 30 seconds

Before the quiz starts, one player is chosen in secret to be the Traitor. Nobody knows who, except them (and the host).

The Traitor quietly steals points, helps some players, hurts others and sends messages to throw you off the scent. Everyone else plays the quiz as normal and tries to work out who it is. Just before half time the whole room votes to banish them, and there is one more vote at the very end.

You might be the Traitor. If you are, a secret screen appears on your phone as soon as you're picked, and a 🗡️ button opens your private menu. If you don't see one, you are a Faithful: your job is to catch whoever does. Keep your phone to yourself.

How the night runs

1
The start
The host picks the Traitor in secret. A short slide tells the room the Traitors are in play. Tap 📖️ Rules on your phone any time to read this again.
2
First half
Answer the questions as normal. The Traitor can steal, gift, block and message in the background. After the first steal, the game pauses after the next question and the big screen tells the room it has happened (who did it, and who they took from, stays secret). Watch the scores for anything that doesn't add up.
3
Shields
Get 3 questions right in a row and you earn a 🛡️ shield. You keep it until you get a question wrong: the Traitor's steals and blocks fail against you while it's on, and you are told what they tried (sharing it is up to you).
4
The Round Table, just before half time
A 3-minute discussion. Talk, accuse, defend, bluff. Then everyone votes on their phone for who to banish (not yourself). The host locks the votes and the player with the most votes goes full screen on camera to tell the room whether they are the Traitor (the Traitor may bluff). Then the host reveals the truth.
5
Half time (10 minutes)
Drinks and a breather. Meanwhile the Traitor (the same one, or a new one if they were caught) answers an honest list of yes/no questions about themselves on their phone. The quiz can't carry on until it's saved, so Traitor, please don't wander off.
6
Second half
Same game. If the vote missed, the same Traitor carries on. If it hit, a new Traitor has been chosen in secret, so the hunt starts again.
7
The final Round Table
At the very end there is one more Round Table: the same vote, the same rules. If a Traitor is found they are banished.
8
The 20 Questions (sometimes)
If two Traitors started the second half, whoever is still standing faces the Traitor's 20 Questions: ask yes/no questions and name them. With a single Traitor all night there is no 20 Questions, so the final Round Table is your only chance.
9
The confessions
Before the reveal, up to five people with their camera on come up on the big screen one at a time. Each looks at the camera and says whether they were a Traitor tonight. The first is a Faithful, and the rest are picked at random, so a Traitor can come up at any point. Do you believe them?
10
The reveal
The big screen shows who the Traitor was, what they got up to, and the final scores. Hidden bonuses are added at this point, so some scores will jump.

Recruiting, and the 20 Questions

If the room misses at half time, the Traitor is offered a recruit: they can pick a player, who chooses to join them (and find out who the Traitor is) or refuse and lose 2,000. Two Traitors share their powers and have to agree each action. If the Traitor is found at half time, the host picks two new Traitors.

If there are two Traitors, each fills in an honest list at half time. After the final Round Table, whoever is still in faces the Traitor's 20 Questions: with both in, one of their two lists is chosen at random for everyone, and finding either Traitor wins.

If you are a Faithful (most of you)

  • Answer well. Everything else rides on top of the normal quiz.
  • Watch the pauses. Every steal, free answer, block and message is a clue to someone's style.
  • Look out for messages. The Traitor can send you a private message (it shows on your phone between questions) or write to the whole room. It might be a bluff.
  • Talk it out. Camera on, say what you noticed at the Round Table. Nobody has to prove anything, it's a vote.

If you are the Traitor

Your 🗡️ button works at any time, before a question or while one is running. One of each power per question, and the menu closes by itself once you have chosen. The powers act on the next question where everyone answers (so one you set during a question counts on the next one) (not on the group games like Taskmaster or Hot Potato).

PowerHow it works
💰 StealTake 100 to 1,000 points from one player and give them to someone else, or keep them yourself (but points you give yourself are only for show: see below). Up to 3 times each half, and from the same player once every 15 questions. A steal on a player with a 🛡️ shield fails (and still counts). The Traitor can't see who has one, and a failed steal or block tells both of you what was tried.
🎁 Free answer (3 a half)Pick a player before a question: they score full marks whatever they answer. You can pick yourself, but points you give yourself are only for show (see below). The big screen shows who was gifted, never who did it.
🚫 Block (3 a half)Keep a player out of the next question. You can block yourself, to look innocent.
✨ Double (3 a half)Anyone's points on the next question count twice, yourself included (for show again if it's you).
✋ HoldAutomatic on one phone: while you are in your 🗡 menu choosing a steal, a power or a message, the host's secret phone shows "HOLD ON" so they do not rush the next question. It goes the moment you make your choice or leave the menu. Nothing shows on the big screen: only the host's own phone is told. With two phones the automatic hold is off, and a ✋ Hold button inside the menu does it by hand (tap it again, or send something, to let go).
📜 MessagesA short secret message to one player, or a message to the whole room. It arrives as a sealed letter that cracks open and unfolds on every screen. Steals and powers can also arrive as a letter: the Traitor chooses whether the whole room sees it or only the person it happened to. A private letter starts "Dear (your name),". Letters come up when the host clicks on, never in the middle of a screen, and ones about a free answer, a block or a double come before the question they are about.
Got another player with you? Team mode works for the Traitor too. Your teammate types your TEAM code (see Part 1) on their phone to secretly join, so one of you can do the answering while the other does the MURDERING! Tap Open the door in your 🗡 menu (Team tab), let them in, then choose which phone answers and which does the Traitor actions. It is still one player and one name on the scoreboard. The answering phone is alerted to every decision the other phone makes.
Points you give yourself are only for show. A steal, a free answer or a double on yourself pushes your score up on the scoreboard all night, which is great for looking innocent (or looking guilty, if you want to bluff). But those extra points don't count: they are taken off your score at the very end.
If there are two Traitors, be honest. Your phone opens a list of yes/no questions about you at half time. Tick the ones that are true of you, and add your own if you like. It becomes the clue list for the 20 Questions, so the quiz stays fair. Be sneaky all night, but not here.
If you are banished, you lose your power and 5,000 points, and a new Traitor is chosen in secret. Say nothing, and enjoy watching them try.
