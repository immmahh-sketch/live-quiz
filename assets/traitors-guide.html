The Traitors

How tonight's quiz works. Read it once, then enjoy the game.

The idea in 30 seconds

Tonight's quiz has a secret. Before it starts, one player has been chosen to be the Traitor. Nobody knows who, except them.

The Traitor will quietly steal points, help some players and hurt others, and send messages to throw you off the scent. Everyone else has to work out who it is and banish them at half time. Play the quiz as normal: answer well, watch the scores, and keep your eyes open.

You might be the Traitor. If you are, a secret screen will tell you so on your phone, with a 🗡 button that opens your private menu. If you don't see one, you are a Faithful: your job is to catch whoever does.

If you are a Faithful (most of you)

1
Answer the questions. Everything else rides on top of the normal quiz.
2
Watch for the announcements. When the Traitor steals points, the game stops after the next question and the big screen tells everyone. You won't see who did it, or who they took from, unless you work it out.
3
Earn a 🛡 shield. Get 3 questions right in a row and you earn a shield. It protects you from the Traitor until you get a question wrong: no stealing from you, no blocking you.
4
Look out for messages. The Traitor can send you a secret message (it appears on your phone between questions) or write to the whole room (a “message from the Traitor” on the big screen). Can you work out who it is? Remember: it might be a bluff.
5
The Round Table. After the last question before half time there is a 3-minute discussion. Talk, accuse, defend. Then everyone votes on their phone for who to banish. The player with the most votes goes full screen on camera and tells the room whether they are the Traitor.
6
The final Round Table, and the 20 Questions. There is one more vote at the very end. If two Traitors started the second half, whoever is still standing then faces the Traitors 20 Questions: ask yes/no questions to find out who they are.

The player with the most votes is put on camera, full screen, and says whether they are the Traitor (the Traitor may bluff!). Then the host reveals the truth. If the vote is tied, nobody is chosen.

Recruiting and the 20 Questions

  • If the room misses at half time, the Traitor is offered a recruit. The person they pick can join (and learns who the Traitor is) or refuse and lose 2,000. Two Traitors share their powers and must agree each action.
  • If the Traitor is found at half time, the host picks two new Traitors.
  • With two Traitors, each fills in an honest list. After the final Round Table the Traitor(s) still in face the 20 Questions: everyone else asks yes/no questions and can guess a name. With both in, one of their two lists is chosen at random for everyone, and finding either Traitor wins.
  • Find a Traitor: +3,000 points. If most of the room finds them, that Traitor loses 2,000. If they get away with it, they win a 5,000 bonus.
  • With a single Traitor all night there is no 20 Questions: the final Round Table is the only chance to catch them.

If you are the Traitor

You have a 🗡 button on your phone, shown only to you. Its menu works at any time, before a question or while one is running. A power you set while a question is running counts on the next question, not the current one. Please keep your phone to yourself.

PowerHow it works
💰 StealTake 100 to 1,000 points from one player and give them to someone else, or keep them yourself (but points you give yourself are only for show, and come off at the end). You can steal up to 5 times each half, and from the same player once every 15 questions. A player with a 🛡 shield can't be robbed. After a steal, the game pauses after the next question and the room is told it has happened.
🎁 Free answer ×5 a halfPick a player before a question starts. Their phone pings “The Traitor has gifted you full points for this question”, and they score full marks whatever they answer. You can pick yourself. After the question the big screen shows who was gifted (never who did it).
🚫 Block ×5 a halfPick a player before a question starts to keep them out of that question. You can block yourself, to look innocent. The big screen shows who was blocked.
✨ Double ×5 a halfPick anyone, yourself included: their points on the next question count twice.
✋ HoldAutomatic on one phone: while you are in your 🗡 menu choosing a steal, a power or a message, the host's secret phone shows "HOLD ON" so they do not rush the next question. It goes the moment you make your choice or leave the menu. Nothing shows on the big screen: only the host's own phone is told. With two phones the automatic hold is off, and a ✋ Hold button inside the menu does it by hand (tap it again, or send something, to let go).
📜 MessagesSend a short secret message to one player (it appears between questions), or a message to everyone. It arrives as a sealed letter that unfolds on the big screen and every phone. The Traitor gets message starters to fill in.

You can use one of each power per question. The powers act on the next question where everyone answers (not on the group games).

With two Traitors: a list of questions about you opens on your phone at half time. Be honest. Tick the ones that are true of you and add your own if you like. It becomes the clue list for the 20 Questions, so the quiz stays fair.
Got another player with you? Use the game code with TEAM in front of it (it is on your screen) on their phone to secretly join (still as one team) so one of you can do the answering while the other does the MURDERING! Tap Open the door in your 🗡 menu (Team tab), let them in, then choose which phone answers and which does the Traitor actions. It is still one player and one name on the scoreboard. The answering phone is alerted to every decision the other phone makes.
Points you give yourself are only for show. A steal, a free answer or a double on yourself looks real on the scoreboard all night, but it is taken off your score at the very end. Use it to look innocent, not to win.
Hidden bonuses. Your +2,000 for escaping the vote and your +5,000 for getting away in the final round are only added at the very end, so nobody sees your score jump.
