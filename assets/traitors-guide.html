The Traitors

How tonight's quiz works. Read it once, then enjoy the game.

The idea in 30 seconds

Tonight's quiz has a secret. Before it starts, one player has been chosen to be the Traitor. Nobody knows who, except them.

The Traitor will quietly steal points, help some players and hurt others, and send messages to throw you off the scent. Everyone else has to work out who it is and banish them at half time. Play the quiz as normal: answer well, watch the scores, and keep your eyes open.

You might be the Traitor. If you are, a secret screen will tell you so on your phone, with a 🗡 button that opens your private menu. If you don't see one, you are a Faithful: your job is to catch whoever does.

If you are a Faithful (most of you)

1
Answer the questions. Everything else rides on top of the normal quiz.
2
Watch for the announcements. When the Traitor steals points, the game stops after the next question and the big screen tells everyone. You won't see who did it, or who they took from, unless you work it out.
3
Earn a 🛡 shield. Get 3 questions right in a row and you earn a shield. It protects you from the Traitor for the next question: no stealing from you, no blocking you.
4
Look out for messages. The Traitor can send you a secret message (it appears on your phone between questions) or write to the whole room (a “message from the Traitor” on the big screen). Can you work out who it is? Remember: it might be a bluff.
5
The Round Table. After the last question before half time there is a 3-minute discussion. Talk, accuse, defend. Then everyone votes on their phone for who to banish.
6
The last round. After half time the Traitor has answered an honest list of questions about themselves. In the last round of the night you play Traitors 20 Questions: ask yes/no questions about the Traitor to find out who they are.

The player with the most votes is the one banished. If the vote is tied, nobody is banished.

The last round: Traitors 20 Questions

  • Everyone except the Traitor asks yes/no questions about the Traitor, and can guess their name at any time.
  • Find the Traitor: +2,000 points.
  • If most of the room finds them, the Traitor loses 2,000.
  • If the Traitor gets away with it, they win a 5,000 bonus.

If you are the Traitor

You have a 🗡 button on your phone, shown only to you. Its menu works in the break between questions. Please keep your phone to yourself.

PowerHow it works
💰 StealTake 100 to 1,000 points from one player and give them to someone else (never yourself). You can steal once every 5 questions, and from the same player once every 15 questions. A player with a 🛡 shield can't be robbed. After a steal, the game pauses after the next question and the room is told it has happened.
🎁 Free answer ×5Pick a player before a question starts. Their phone pings “The Traitor has gifted you full points for this question”, and they score full marks whatever they answer. You can pick yourself. After the question the big screen shows who was gifted (never who did it).
🚫 Block ×5Pick a player before a question starts to keep them out of that question. You can block yourself, to look innocent. The big screen shows who was blocked.
✨ Double ×5Pick anyone, yourself included: their points on the next question count twice.
📜 MessagesSend a short secret message to one player (it appears between questions), or a message to everyone (“a message from the Traitor” on the big screen).

You can use one of each power per question. The powers act on the next question where everyone answers (not on the group games).

At half time: a list of questions about you opens on your phone. Be honest. Tick the ones that are true of you and add your own if you like. It becomes the clue list for the last round, so the quiz stays fair.
Hidden bonuses. Your +2,000 for escaping the vote and your +5,000 for getting away in the final round are only added at the very end, so nobody sees your score jump.
