Let's Quiz! Players' Guide

How every game works and how you score. All you need is your phone and something to shout at the telly.

1 · JoinGo to letsquiz.uk/play (or scan the QR code on the big screen) and type the game code shown on screen.
2 · Pick a name and emojiThat's you on the scoreboard. Keep the page open: your phone is your buzzer, keypad and answer pad.
3 · Watch the big screenQuestions appear on the TV, and you answer on your phone. After each one you'll see who got it and the scores.

How scoring works

instant: 1000buzzer: 500

Most questions pay for speed. A right answer is worth 1000 points if you answer instantly, sliding evenly down to 500 if you answer as the clock runs out. A wrong answer, or no answer, scores nothing and never loses points, unless a game below says otherwise. Games with their own scoring explain it in their panel below.

💡 These are the standard settings. The host can change points, timers and targets for their own quiz, and the screen always shows what a question is worth before you play.

The quick-fire classics

🔤Multiple choice

Four answers on your phone. Tap the right one. 20 seconds.

Right: 500–1000, faster is more.

✅True or false

Is the statement true or false? Tap your answer. 15 seconds, so be quick.

Right: 500–1000, faster is more.

✎Type the answer

Type your answer on your phone. Close spellings count, and anything borderline gets a fair check. 30 seconds.

Right: 500–1000, faster is more.

🔢Put in order

Drag the items into the right order (oldest first, biggest first and so on), then lock it in. 45 seconds.

All in the right order: 500–1000. If the host switches on part marks, you get a share for each item in the right place.

🔗Match up

Tap an item, then tap its partner, until everything is paired. 45 seconds.

All pairs right: 500–1000 (a share for each right pair if part marks are on).

🗂️Categorise

Put each answer into the right group. 45 seconds.

All sorted right: 500–1000 (a share for each one if part marks are on).

📍Drop the pin

A map on your phone. Work out the answer and drop your pin on it. 25 seconds.

Pin on (or very near) the spot: 500–1000 for speed. The further away, the more that shrinks, down to nothing if you're way off. The closest pin gets a shout on screen.

🎯Nearest wins

A question with a number for an answer. Type your best guess. 25 seconds, and speed doesn't matter here.

Up to 1000 for how close you are, plus a +250 bonus for the closest guess (shared if tied).

Word and picture puzzles

🔀Answer Smash

Name the picture, answer the clue, then smash the two together where they overlap: Brad Pitt + Pittsburgh = Brad Pittsburgh. 30 seconds.

The full smash, right: 500–1000.

🎡Wheel of Fortune

A hidden phrase and its category. Letters turn over one at a time. Solve it on your phone whenever you can. 60 seconds.

Right: 500–1000. The sooner you solve it (fewer letters showing), the more you score.

🎤Rhyme Time

Two clues whose answers rhyme. Type both answers, in either order. 30 seconds.

Both right: 500–1000. One on its own doesn't count.

🧩Dingbats

Say what you see: a well-known phrase hidden in how the words are laid out. 45 seconds.

Right: 500–1000.

🗯️Catchphrase

A clip plays on the big screen. Say what you see: type the well-known saying it shows. 50 seconds.

Right: 500–1000.

🎵Name That Tune

Listen to the clip and answer what's asked: the song, the artist, the film it's from, the next line, or the year. 30 seconds. Sometimes the clip plays backwards!

Right: 500–1000. For the year, anything within the allowed range counts.

🧩Picture Reveal

A famous face hidden behind tiles that flip over one by one (or under a blur that slowly clears). Type who it is on your phone as soon as you know. 40 seconds.

Right: 500–1000. The sooner you get it (fewer tiles gone), the more you score.

☝️Only One

Everyone answers the same prompt, like "Name a pizza topping". Be the only one to say it! If anyone else gives the same answer, you're both out, and so is a blank. It has to be a genuine answer: the host can kick "Shoes". The rest go through to the next prompt. 25 seconds a prompt.

100 for every round you survive, and 1000 for the last one standing (shared if the prompts run out).

🎓Highbrow Lowbrow

A hard clue on screen. Stuck? Tap your phone for the easy clue, but it halves your prize. 40 seconds.

Right on the hard clue: 1000 flat. Right after asking for the easy clue: 500. Speed doesn't matter.

🧠The 1% Club

No knowledge needed, just logic. The screen shows what percentage of people get it. 30 seconds.

A flat prize: the rarer, the richer. 1100 − 10 × the percentage, between 200 and 1000.

The big games

💥Wipeout

A board full of answers to one question, and some of them are wrong. Everyone gets 20 seconds to study the board, then you take turns to pick one answer you think is right. You have 5 seconds on your turn.

Each right pick: +200, and play moves to the next player. Pick a wrong one: −100 and you're out of this board, while everyone else plays on. Run out of time: you're out, but you lose nothing. The board ends when every right answer has been found, or every wrong answer has been picked, or everyone is out.

🏁The Race

Quick-fire questions on your phone, all at your own pace, while your emoji races across the screen. First to 10 right wins. 10 wrong and you're out of the race. 2 minutes.

Every right answer: +100. Winner: +500 (first to 10, or the clear leader when time's up). Second: +200. Third: +100. With four or more runners, finishing last on your own costs −200.

💣Hot Potato

A lit bomb goes round the room, and nobody knows how long the fuse is (somewhere between 40 and 90 seconds). If it's in your hands, answer the question on your phone. Right, and you choose who gets it next. Wrong, and you get another question. General-knowledge questions unless the host picks a topic.

Each right answer while holding it: +50. Holding it when it goes BOOM: −300.

👑King of the Hill

A fastest-finger question picks two players to go head to head. Buzz on your phone, then say your answer out loud (you get 3 seconds). Get it right and you're on the hill and win a hill point. At most 12 head-to-heads. General knowledge unless a topic is set.

Every hill point: +100. First to 3 hill points is King of the Hill: +1000 bonus. If nobody gets there, hill points still pay.

🏃The Chase

Whoever is leading the quiz becomes the Chaser. Everyone else is one team, starting 2 steps ahead. On each question, the first right answer moves that side one step: the team towards home, the Chaser towards the team. The team needs 5 steps to get home. General knowledge unless a topic is set.

Team gets home: +1000 to every team player. Chaser catches them: +200 to the Chaser.

⬢Blockbusters

Two sides, half the players on each (once one is full you join the other). Your side picks a letter, and the answer starts with that letter. First to type it right wins the hexagon for their side. Join opposite edges of the board (left to right, or top to bottom) to win.

Each hexagon you win: +50 to you. The side that links up: +500 to every player on it.

🕵️20 Questions

Everyone has the same mystery person or thing. Tap a question and your phone says yes or no, with new questions opening up as you go. Guess whenever you like, but a wrong guess uses up a question. 20 questions, 3 minutes.

First to crack it: +1000. Second: +500. Third: +100. Still stuck at the end, or out of questions: −200.

🎨Draw It

When it's your turn, pick a word and draw it on your phone (no letters or numbers!). Everyone else types guesses as often as they like. Letters of the word appear as the clock runs down. Usually three drawings, 60 seconds each.

Guessers: 150–500, the quicker the better. The artist: +100 for every player who guesses their drawing.

Good to know

⏭Skipping

Some questions let you skip. A skip scores nothing and costs nothing.

🔌Dropped out?

Open letsquiz.uk/play again on the same phone and join with the same code. You come back as yourself, score and all.

📉Where you can lose points

Only in Wipeout (wrong pick), The Race (last place), Hot Potato (holding the bomb) and 20 Questions (still stuck). Everywhere else, a wrong answer just scores nothing.

✨Double points

The last round of the night is usually double points: everything you score in it counts twice. It says so on the round card.

🏆The winner

Points add up across every round. The scoreboard shows after each question, and the final podium at the end.
